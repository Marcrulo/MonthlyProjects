# [](#prologue)Prologue
I really love coding. Coding is both my profession and my hobby, but unfortunately, I haven't found time to commit to any interesting hobby projects as of late. My excuse is usually that I don't have time, or that the project becomes to large and messy. 

But I intend to try a _new approach_: 
> **_"I have to complete and document a coding project each month_**.
> **_But... should I happen to come across a different creative non-coding project, the same rules apply._"** 

This way, I must do some project, and then figure out the size of it, depending on how much time I have available. In reality there is never a good moment to start a project - _just do it_.
