## [](#prologue)Prologue
I have played the piano most of my life, but I have always preferred listening more energetic rock music, which rarely includes piano. Learning to play the guitar would probably have been more fitting - but I just really loved *playing* the piano. I guess playing and listening are not the same. The closest thing I ever got to playing guitar was to play a ton of Guitar Hero - a game I really adore. I recently realized that I could combine my love for playing piano with my love for energetic music and Guitar Hero.

My solution was to create ***Keytar Hero***...
